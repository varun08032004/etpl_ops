## A. PROGRAMME OVERVIEW

### Duration & Workload
| Parameter | Value | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| **Total Programme Duration** | 16 weeks | Sufficient depth for 12-course progression with practical integration |
| **Weekly Workload** | 6–8 hours | Sustainable alongside work; allows 2–3 lessons/week + practice |
| **Total Learning Hours** | ~100 hours | Covers 12 courses × ~8 hours + final project |
| **Number of Courses** | 12 core + 1 final project | Aligned with 12-level progression |
| **Number of Modules** | ~36 modules | ~3 modules/course |
| **Number of Lessons** | ~120 lessons | ~10 lessons/module; ~45 min each |

### Target Audience
- **Primary**: All EtherTrack employees (mixed technical/business backgrounds)
- **Prerequisites**: None (begins at fundamentals)
- **Assumed Carbon Knowledge**: Beginner

### Certification Levels
| Level | Name | Requirements |
|-------|------|--------------|
| 1 | Carbon Foundations | Courses 01–03 + assessments |
| 2 | Carbon Operations | Courses 01–06 + practical assignments |
| 3 | Carbon Project Analyst | Courses 01–09 + project analysis |
| 4 | EtherTrack Carbon Specialist | All courses + final project + EtherTrack track |

## C. COURSE DETAILS

### COURSE 01: Carbon & Climate Science
**Purpose**: Establish scientific foundation for why carbon markets exist.
**Target Competency**: Explain the physical basis of climate change, carbon cycle, and greenhouse effect.
**Duration**: 8 hours (3 modules × ~2.5h + 1h assessment)
**Prerequisites**: None

**Modules**:
| Module | Title | Duration | Learning Objectives |
|--------|-------|----------|---------------------|
| 1.1 | Greenhouse Effect & Carbon Cycle | 2.5h | Explain radiative forcing; trace carbon through reservoirs; calculate CO2e from CH4/N2O |
| 1.2 | Climate Change Impacts & Mitigation | 2.5h | Describe temperature targets (1.5°C/2°C); link emissions to warming; distinguish mitigation/adaptation |
| 1.3 | International Climate Policy | 2.5h | Trace UNFCCC → Kyoto → Paris; explain NDCs; distinguish voluntary vs compliance |

**Practical Exercises**:
- Calculate CO2e for a mixed-gas emission source
- Map a country's NDC to sectoral targets

**Assessment**: 15 questions (5 conceptual, 5 calculation, 5 case-based) | Pass: 70%
