## 4. Pilot Workload Recommendation

### Recommended: 7 hours/week for pilot
**Rationale:**
- 5 hrs/week is too slow (3-7 months for full tracks)
- 10 hrs/week is too aggressive for working professionals
- 7 hrs/week balances realistic completion with pilot timeline

### Pilot Duration Calculation

| Cohort Type | Track | Hours | Weeks @ 7 hrs/wk | Pilot Calendar |
|-------------|-------|-------|------------------|----------------|
| **Management** | Common Core (69.5) | 10 weeks | 2.5 months |
| **Carbon Operations** | Carbon Ops (123.5) | 18 weeks | 4.5 months |
| **Engineering** | Common + Eng (147.5) | 21 weeks | 5.0 months |
| **Compliance** | Common + Compliance (141.5) | 20 weeks | 4.8 months |
| **Finance** | Common + Finance (143.5) | 20.5 weeks | 4.8 months |
| **Product/Sales** | Common + Track (81.5-85.5) | 12 weeks | 2.8 months |

### Recommended Pilot Duration: **20 weeks (5 months)**
- Covers up to Finance/Compliance tracks
- Allows buffer for holidays, sick leave, project work
- Aligns with programme duration_weeks = 16 (blueprint) + buffer

## 6. Workload Risk Assessment

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Employees cannot dedicate 7 hrs/week | Medium | High | Flexible deadlines; allow 8-10 week buffer |
| 119.5 hrs too much for 16-week pilot | Medium | High | Extend to 20 weeks; prioritize Common Core |
| Capstone (15 hrs) too compressed | High | Medium | Schedule capstone in final 4 weeks |
| Assessment burden (11 hrs) | Low | Medium | Spread assessments across weeks |
| Practical exercises (37 hrs) require focus time | Medium | Medium | Block calendar; manager support |

---

## 7. Recommended Pilot Timeline

```
Week 1-2:     Onboarding, baseline assessment, C01-C02
Week 3-4:     C03-C04
Week 5-6:     C05, C13
```
