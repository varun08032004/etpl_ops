### 16.4.1.2 Section A: Science & Policy (25%) — Sample Questions

**Multiple Choice (2 pts each):**
1. The radiative forcing of CO2 is approximately logarithmic with concentration because:
   A) CO2 absorbs all outgoing IR radiation
   B) Absorption bands saturate; forcing increases in the wings
   C) CO2 concentration grows exponentially
   D) Water vapor feedback is linear

2. The GWP100 of CH4 (AR6) is 27.9. This means:
   A) 1 tonne CH4 = 27.9 tonnes CO2 over 100 years
   B) CH4 is 27.9x more potent than CO2 per molecule
   C) CH4 stays in atmosphere 27.9x longer than CO2
   D) CH4 causes 27.9x more warming per kg emitted

3. India's CCTS compliance cycle surrender deadline is:
   A) March 31
   B) April 30
   C) May 31
   D) June 30

**Short Answer (5 pts each):**
4. Explain the difference between "Corresponding Adjustment" under Article 6.2 and "Corresponding Adjustment" under CORSIA in 3 sentences.
5. Why does the GHG Protocol require reporting both location-based and market-based Scope 2 emissions?

### 16.4.1.3 Section B: Technical & Financial (35%) — Calculations

**Problem 1: Breakeven Carbon Price (15 pts)**
A 100 MW solar plant in Rajasthan:
- Capex: INR 450 Cr
- PPA: ₹2.75/kWh × 25 yr (SECI)
- Capacity: 100 MW AC; CUF: 22%; Degradation: 0.7%/yr
- Opex: INR 1.5 Cr/yr (5% escalation)
- Debt: 70% @ 10.5% (15 yr, sculpted); Min DSCR 1.2x
- WACC: 10.5%; Equity Hurdle: 16%
- Project Life: 25 yr; Carbon Revenue = ERs × Carbon Price × (1 - Buffer)

**Task:** Calculate the NPV Breakeven Carbon Price ($/tCO2e) using the analytical approximation. Show your work.

**Problem 2: ERPA Pricing (10 pts)**
A buyer offers: 10-yr ERPA, 80% of ERs at $14/t (fixed), 20% at spot.
Project: 50 MW wind, 120k tCO2e/yr, 25-yr life.
Your hurdle: Equity IRR ≥ 16%.
Current spot: $12/t. Forward curve: $13 (yr1), $14 (yr2), $15 (yr3+).
**Task:** Should you accept? Show the IRR comparison.

### 16.4.2.3 Defense Strategy — Handling Questions

**Question Categories & Response Framework:**

| Question Type | Framework | Example |
|---------------|-----------|---------|
| **Challenge Assumption** | "You assume X. What if Y?" | ACKNOWLEDGE → EVIDENCE → IMPACT → MITIGATION |
| **Challenge Number** | "Your IRR is only 20 bps above hurdle." | ACKNOWLEDGE → CONTEXT → SENSITIVITY → MITIGATION |
| **Challenge Risk** | "What if SECI delays payment?" | ACKNOWLEDGE → PROBABILITY × IMPACT → MITIGATION → CONTINGENCY |
| **Challenge Methodology** | "Why ACM0002 not AMS-I.D?" | CRITERIA → FIT → TRADE-OFF |
| **Challenge Assumption** | "Carbon price at $15 is aggressive." | RANGE → BASE CASE SOURCE → SENSITIVITY → FLOOR |
| **Strategic** | "Why not add storage?" | TRADE-OFF → NPV IMPACT → OPTION VALUE |

**Response Template (STAR-C):**
```
S — Situation: "The question addresses [topic]..."
T — Task: "The key consideration is [core issue]..."
A — Action: "Our analysis shows [evidence/data]..."
R — Result: "This means [conclusion/impact]..."
C — Caveat: "The key sensitivity is [X]; we mitigate by [Y]..."
```

### 16.4.2.3 Common Trap Questions & Prepared Responses

| Trap Question | Bad Response | Good Response |
|---------------|--------------|---------------|
| "Your IRR is barely above hurdle." | "It meets the hurdle!" | "Base case clears by 20 bps. Bear case at $8/t still covers senior debt. We priced a $12 floor in ERPA." |
| "What if carbon price crashes to $5?" | "Unlikely." | "Bear case at $8 still clears DSCR. ERPA floor at $12 protects downside. We've stress-tested to $5." |
| "Why not add battery storage?" | "Too expensive." | "Storage adds INR 2.5 Cr/MW. NPV impact -INR 15 Cr at current tariffs. We've modeled as option (Year 5 decision gate)." |
| "Your model has hardcodes." | "Only a few." | "External audit completed (FAST compliant). Zero hardcodes in calc engine. All inputs on Assumptions tab." |
| "What if SECI delays payment 120 days?" | "PPA is sovereign." | "DSRA covers 9 months. Parent guarantee backs SECI obligation. Policy stress test shows NPV > 0 at 120-day delay." |

### 16.4.2.3 Defense Etiquette & Psychology

**Do's:**
- ✅ Listen fully before answering
- ✅ Pause 2-3 seconds before answering (shows thought)
- ✅ Admit uncertainty: "That's a great question. My current view is X, though Y is a valid concern."
- ✅ Refer to your model: "As shown in Cell B45 of the Assumptions tab..."
- ✅ Acknowledge valid criticism: "Fair point. We modeled that as a sensitivity..."

**Don'ts:**
- ❌ Don't get defensive or argumentative
- ❌ Don't guess — say "I'd need to check the model" rather than guess
- ❌ Don't read slides verbatim
- ❌ Don't blame the model, data, or team
- ❌ Don't ramble — 60-90 seconds per answer max
