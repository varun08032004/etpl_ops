# C12: Carbon Project Economics
## Module 12.1: Cost Structure & Revenue Modeling (3 lessons x 40min = 2h)

### Lesson 12.1.1: CAPEX/OPEX Breakdown by Project Type
**Lesson Code:** C12.1.1
**Duration:** 40 minutes
**Lesson Type:** document
**Tier:** professional

**Learning Objectives:**
1. Decompose CAPEX and OPEX for major carbon project types (renewables, forestry, cookstoves, industrial) (Bloom: Apply)
2. Identify cost drivers, benchmarks, and contingency practices for each type (Bloom: Analyze)
3. Build a project-type-specific cost template for financial modeling (Bloom: Create)

**Prerequisites:** C06.1.1 (Project Types & Technologies), C07.2.1 (Baseline Methodologies)

**Why This Matters:**
Cost structure determines project viability. A solar project's economics are driven by capex and capacity factor; a cookstove project by distribution cost and usage rate; a forestry project by land cost and buffer pool. Understanding type-specific cost structures lets you build credible financial models, negotiate ERPAs, and spot inflated budgets in due diligence.

**Core Concept: Cost Structure as Project DNA — Each Type Has Its Own Economics**

### 12.1.1.1 Cost Structure Framework — Universal Categories

| Category | Sub-Categories | Typical % of Total Cost |
|----------|----------------|-------------------------|
| **CAPEX (Upfront)** | Equipment, Civil Works, Engineering, Permits, Land, Grid Connection, Contingency | 60-85% (varies by type) |
| **OPEX (Annual)** | Operations, Maintenance, Monitoring, Verification, Insurance, Admin, Land Lease | 5-15% of CAPEX/yr |
| **CARBON-SPECIFIC** | Validation, Verification (recurring), Registry Fees, Buffer Pool, MRV, DOE/VVB | 1-5% of revenue |
| **FINANCING** | Interest, Fees, DSCR Reserve, Hedging | Project finance dependent |
| **TRANSACTION** | Legal, Advisory, ERPA Negotiation, Due Diligence | 1-3% of deal value |

### 12.1.1.2 Renewable Energy (Solar PV / Wind) — Cost Structure

**Solar PV — Typical CAPEX Breakdown (Utility-Scale, India 2024):**
| Component | % of CAPEX | Benchmark (INR Cr/MW) | Key Drivers |
|-----------|------------|----------------------|-------------|
| **Modules** | 45-50% | 2.0-2.5 | Technology (mono/bi-facial), vendor, volume |
| **Inverters** | 8-10% | 0.4-0.6 | String vs central, warranty |
| **Structure/Mounting** | 10-12% | 0.5-0.7 | Fixed vs tracker, terrain |
| **Civil & Electrical** | 12-15% | 0.6-0.9 | Site conditions, grid distance |
| **Engineering & Permits** | 3-5% | 0.2-0.3 | EPC margin, regulatory |
| **Land** | 5-10% | 0.3-0.8 | Location, acquisition model |
| **Grid Connection** | 5-8% | 0.3-0.5 | Substation, evacuation |
| **Contingency** | 3-5% | 0.2-0.3 | EPC contract type |

**Total Solar CAPEX: ~INR 4.5-5.5 Cr/MW (2024)**

**Wind — Typical CAPEX Breakdown:**
| Component | % of CAPEX | Benchmark (INR Cr/MW) |
|-----------|------------|----------------------|
| **Turbines** | 65-70% | 4.5-5.5 |
| **Civil (Foundations, Roads)** | 10-15% | 0.8-1.2 |
| **Electrical (Collection, Substation)** | 8-10% | 0.6-0.8 |
| **Engineering, Permits, Land** | 8-10% | 0.6-0.8 |

| **Wind** | 2.0-3.0% | Turbine maintenance (OEM contract), gearbox, blade inspection |

### 12.1.1.3 Forestry / Nature-Based — Cost Structure

**ARR (Afforestation/Reforestation) — CAPEX:**
| Component | % | Notes |
|-----------|---|-------|
| **Land Acquisition/Lease** | 30-50% | Major variable; lease vs purchase |
| **Site Prep & Planting** | 25-35% | Seedlings, labor, machinery |
| **Species Selection/Nursery** | 5-10% | Native vs commercial |
| **Fencing/Protection** | 5-10% | Fire, grazing, encroachment |
| **Community/Benefit Sharing** | 5-10% | FPIC, agreements |
| **Monitoring Setup** | 3-5% | Plots, equipment, training |
| **Validation/Registration** | 2-3% | VVB, standard body fees |

**REDD+ (Avoided Deforestation) — CAPEX:**
| Component | % | Notes |
|-----------|---|-------|
| **Land Rights/Legal** | 20-30% | Tenure clarification, legal |
| **Baseline & Reference Region** | 15-20% | Analytics, field plots |
| **Community Engagement/FPIC** | 20-30% | Ongoing, not one-time |
| **Monitoring & Enforcement** | 15-20% | Patrols, satellite, tech |
| **Buffer Pool (Opportunity Cost)** | 10-15% | Credits withheld |

**Forestry OPEX (Annual):**
- 5-10% of CAPEX (higher for REDD+ enforcement)
- Key: Monitoring (satellite + ground), community payments, fire management, verification

### 12.1.1.6 Carbon-Specific Costs (All Project Types)

| Cost Item | Frequency | Typical Range | Notes |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|-------|

### 12.1.1.7 Contingency & Escalation Practices

| Practice | Standard | Application |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| **CAPEX Contingency** | 5-10% (EPC wrap: 3-5%) | Higher for greenfield, complex sites |
| **OPEX Escalation** | 3-5%/yr (India: 5-6%) | CPI-linked; separate for labor vs materials |
| **Carbon Price Escalation** | Scenario-dependent | Not in base case; scenario only |
| **Generation Degradation** | Solar: 0.5-0.8%/yr; Wind: 1-2%/yr | Affects revenue, not cost |
| **Insurance** | 0.5-1.5% of CAPEX/yr | Property, BI, political risk |

### Lesson 12.1.2: Revenue Streams: Credits, Energy, Co-Benefits
**Lesson Code:** C12.1.2
**Duration:** 40 minutes
**Lesson Type:** document
**Tier:** professional

**Learning Objectives:**
1. Model all revenue streams for carbon projects: carbon credits, energy sales, co-benefits (Bloom: Apply)
2. Structure revenue assumptions: pricing, volume, timing, contract types (Bloom: Create)
3. Assess revenue quality: contracted vs merchant, currency, counterparty risk (Bloom: Evaluate)

**Prerequisites:** C12.1.1, C10.1.1 (Registry Types), C14.1.1 (Market Participants)

**Why This Matters:**
Carbon revenue is rarely the only revenue — and often not the largest. A solar project's PPA may bring 70% of revenue; a cookstove project may have zero energy revenue but high carbon revenue; a forestry project may have sustainable timber or NTFPs. Modeling all streams correctly, with their distinct risk profiles, is essential for credible NPV/IRR.

**Core Concept: Revenue Stack — Carbon as One Layer in a Multi-Stream Model**

### 12.1.2.1 Revenue Stream Taxonomy

| Stream | Project Types | Contract Type | Risk Profile |
|--------|---------------|---------------|--------------|
| **Carbon Credits** | All | ERPA (forward), Spot, OTC | Policy, price, issuance risk |
| **Energy/Power** | Renewables, Industrial | PPA, FIT, Merchant, Captive | Offtaker, curtailment, price |
| **Fuel/Commodity** | Biogas, Landfill Gas, Industrial | Offtake, Spot | Commodity price, quality |
| **Co-Benefits/SDG** | Forestry, Cookstoves, Community | SDG-labeled ERPA, Grants, CSR | Verification, niche buyers |
| **Byproducts** | Biochar, Compost, Timber | Spot, Contract | Market, quality |
| **Incentives/Subsidies** | All (esp. renewables) | Government schemes | Policy continuity, eligibility |

### 12.1.2.2 Carbon Credit Revenue — Modeling Depth

**Volume Calculation:**
```
Annual Carbon Revenue = ERs (tCO2e/yr) × Carbon Price ($/tCO2e) × (1 - Buffer %)

WHERE:
ERs = Baseline - Project Emissions - Leakage (per methodology)
Buffer % = Methodology/Registry requirement (0-30%)
```

**Pricing Assumptions (Critical):**
| Approach | When Used | Pros | Cons |
|----------|-----------|------|------|
| **Contracted (ERPA)** | Project finance, committed buyers | Certainty; bankable | May leave upside on table |
| **Forward Curve** | Market-linked models | Market-consistent | Curve may be illiquid |
| **Spot + Premium/Discount** | Merchant/portfolio | Flexible | High uncertainty |
| **Scenario-Based** | All models (base/bear/bull) | Captures range | Not a single forecast |

**Carbon Price Scenario Design (2024 Context):**
| Scenario | 2025 | 2027 | 2030 | 2035 | Rationale |
|----------|------|------|------|------|-----------|
| **Bear** | $5 | $8 | $12 | $15 | Oversupply; weak demand; no CCP mandate |
| **Base** | $12 | $18 | $28 | $40 | CCP adoption; corporate net zero; CORSIA |
| **Bull** | $20 | $35 | $60 | $100 | Regulation-driven; Article 6; scarcity |

**Vintage & Label Premiums:**
| Premium | Typical Range | Applies To |
|---------|---------------|------------|
| **CCP-Label** | +10-30% | CCP-eligible credits |
| **CORSIA-Eligible** | +15-40% | Aviation-compliant vintage/methodology |
| **Article 6.2 Authorized** | +20-50% | ITMOs with CA |
| **SDG-Verified** | +5-20% | GS SDG / Verra SD VISta |
| **Removal vs Avoidance** | +50-200% | DACCS, BECCS, ARR vs REDD+ |
| **Vintage (Current vs Old)** | -20-50% (discount) | Pre-2020 vintages |

**Contract Structures:**
| Structure | Volume Risk | Price Risk | Typical Tenor |
|-----------|-------------|------------|---------------|
| **Fixed Volume, Fixed Price** | Buyer | Buyer | 3-7 years |
| **Fixed Volume, Indexed Price** | Buyer | Shared | 5-10 years |
| **Tolling (Project Sells, Buyer Markets)** | Project | Project | 1-3 years |
| **Pre-Payment / Forward** | Shared | Buyer | 1-3 years (pre-pay) |
| **Spot/OTC** | Project | Project | N/A |

### 12.1.2.3 Energy Revenue — PPA & Merchant Modeling

**PPA Structures (India Context):**
| Type | Tariff Basis | Escalation | Tenor | Offtaker Risk |
|------|--------------|------------|-------|---------------|
| **Fixed Tariff** | ₹/kWh fixed | None or fixed % | 25 yrs | Low (if govt/utility) |
| **Indexed Tariff** | Base + CPI/WPI | Annual | 25 yrs | Low |
| **Hybrid (Capacity + Energy)** | ₹/MW/month + ₹/kWh | Partial | 25 yrs | Medium |
| **Group Captive** | Negotiated | Negotiated | 10-15 yrs | Corporate credit |
| **Merchant (Exchange)** | Market clearing | Real-time | N/A | High (price vol) |

**Key PPA Modeling Assumptions:**
| Assumption | Typical Range | Source |
|------------|---------------|--------|
| **Tariff (Solar)** | ₹2.2-3.0/kWh | SECI/State auctions |
| **Tariff (Wind)** | ₹2.5-3.5/kWh | SECI/State auctions |
| **Escalation** | 0-3%/yr | PPA terms |
| **Curtailment** | 1-5% of generation | Grid studies |
| **Availability Guarantee** | 95-98% | PPA penalty clause |
| **Deemed Generation** | For curtailment | PPA clause |

**Merchant Revenue Modeling:**
```
Merchant Revenue = Generation (MWh) × Hourly/Daily Price ($/MWh)
- Requires: Price forecast (fundamental or statistical)
- Risk: High volatility; correlation with carbon price?
- Mitigation: Hedge ratio (typically 70-90% contracted)
```

### 12.1.2.4 Co-Benefits & SDG Revenue

**Monetization Pathways:**
| Pathway | Mechanism | Typical Value | Buyers |
|---------|-----------|---------------|--------|
| **SDG-Labeled Credits** | Premium on carbon credit | +5-20% | Corporates with SDG targets |
| **Separate SDG Credits** | Standalone instruments (e.g., Verra SD VISta) | $1-10/unit | Specialized funds |
| **CSR/Grants** | Direct funding for outcomes | Project-specific | Foundations, govts |
| **Outcome-Based Finance** | Payment per verified outcome (e.g., stove distributed) | $5-50/stove | DFI, govts |

**Modeling Approach:**
- **Conservative:** Exclude from base case; model as upside scenario
- **If Contracted:** Include as separate revenue line with own probability
- **Key Risk:** Verification cost may exceed revenue for small projects

### 12.1.2.5 Revenue Quality Assessment

**Revenue Quality Scorecard:**
| Dimension | High Quality | Medium | Low Quality |
|-----------|--------------|--------|-------------|
| **Contracted %** | >80% | 50-80% | <50% |
| **Offtaker Credit** | Sovereign/Investment Grade | Corporate IG | Sub-IG / Unrated |
| **Tenor** | >15 years | 7-15 years | <7 years |
| **Currency Match** | Local currency (project) | Hard currency (hedged) | Hard currency (unhedged) |
| **Take-or-Pay** | Full | Partial | None |
| **Force Majeure** | Balanced | Buyer-favorable | Project-favorable |

**Revenue Stack Example (50 MW Solar India):**
| Stream | Annual Revenue | % of Total | Quality | Contract Tenor |
|--------|----------------|------------|---------|----------------|
| **PPA (Fixed ₹2.75/kWh)** | ₹48 Cr | 68% | High (SECI, 25yr) | 25 yr |
| **Carbon (Base $15/t, 120k tCO2e)** | ₹15 Cr | 21% | Medium (ERPA 5yr) | 5 yr |
| **REC (If applicable)** | ₹5 Cr | 7% | Medium | 1-3 yr |
| **Generation-Based Incentive** | ₹3 Cr | 4% | Policy-dependent | Annual |

### Lesson 12.1.3: Financial Model Architecture & Assumptions
**Lesson Code:** C12.1.3
**Duration:** 40 minutes
**Lesson Type:** document
**Tier:** professional

**Learning Objectives:**
1. Design a standardized financial model structure for carbon projects (Bloom: Create)
2. Document and justify all key assumptions with sources and ranges (Bloom: Apply)
3. Build assumption tables that enable auditability and scenario switching (Bloom: Create)

**Prerequisites:** C12.1.1, C12.1.2

**Why This Matters:**
A financial model is only as good as its architecture and assumptions. Poor structure makes models unauditable, un-updatable, and error-prone. Undocumented assumptions hide risk. This lesson gives you a professional model template used in project finance and investment committees — with clear separation of inputs, calculations, and outputs.

**Core Concept: Model as Audit Trail — Inputs → Calculations → Outputs → No Hardcodes**

### 12.1.3.1 Model Architecture — Standard Structure

**Workbook/Sheet Structure:**
```
SHEET 1: COVER & DOCUMENTATION
├── Project Info, Model Version, Author, Date, Purpose
├── Assumption Sources & Versions
├── Color Coding Legend (Input/Calc/Output/Link)
└── Version History

SHEET 2: ASSUMPTIONS (All Inputs — NO FORMULAS)
├── 2.1 Technical: Capacity, CUF, Degradation, Availability, Losses
├── 2.2 Carbon: ERs/yr, Buffer %, Vintage, Label Premiums
├── 2.3 Capex: Breakdown, Phasing, Contingency, Escalation
├── 2.4 Opex: Year 1, Escalation, Components, Carbon Costs
├── 2.5 Revenue: PPA, Carbon (3 scenarios), REC, Other
├── 2.6 Financing: Debt/Equity, Interest, Tenor, DSCR, Fees
├── 2.7 Tax: Corporate, MAT, Depreciation, Incentives (80IA, etc.)
├── 2.8 Macro: Inflation (CPI/WPI), FX, Discount Rate (WACC)
└── 2.9 Scenario Switches: Carbon Price, Generation, Capex, Opex

SHEET 3: TECHNICAL CALCULATIONS
├── Generation Profile (Monthly/Annual, 25-30 years)
├── Degradation & Availability Application
├── Gross/Net Generation
├── Carbon: Baseline, Project Emissions, Leakage, Net ERs
└── Buffer Pool Application

SHEET 4: CAPEX & FUNDING
├── Capex Phasing (Monthly/Quarterly during construction)
├── Funding Sources (Debt Drawdown, Equity Injection)
├── IDC (Interest During Construction)
├── DSCR Reserve, DSRA Funding
└── Sources & Uses Statement

SHEET 5: OPERATING STATEMENT (P&L)
├── Revenue (by stream, monthly/annual)
├── OPEX (by category)
├── Carbon Costs (Verification, Registry, Issuance, Buffer)
├── EBITDA
├── Depreciation (Tax & Book)
├── Interest (Senior, Sub, Fees)
├── PBT, Tax (MAT + Normal), PAT

SHEET 6: CASH FLOW STATEMENT
├── CF from Operations (PAT + Non-cash - Tax paid)
├── Debt Service (Principal + Interest + Fees)
├── Capex (Sustaining/Replacement)
├── Working Capital Changes
├── Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF)
├── Free Cash Flow to Equity (FCFE)
├── Distributions (Dividends, Shareholder Loans)
└── Cash Balance & DSCR Calculation

SHEET 7: DEBT SCULPTING & SCHEDULE
├── Repayment Profile (Annuity / Sculpted to CF)
├── Interest Calculation (Floating/Fixed, Hedging)
├── DSCR / LLCR / PLCR per Period
├── Covenant Tracking

SHEET 8: RETURNS & METRICS
├── Project IRR (FCFF) / Equity IRR (FCFE)
├── NPV @ WACC / Equity NPV @ Cost of Equity
├── Payback (Simple / Discounted)
├── LCOE (Levelized Cost of Energy)
├── LCOC (Levelized Cost of Carbon)
├── Sensitivity Table (Key Drivers)
└── Scenario Comparison (Base/Bear/Bull)

SHEET 9: SENSITIVITY & SCENARIOS
├── Tornado Chart Data (Single-variable)
├── Two-Variable Sensitivity (e.g., Carbon Price × Generation)
├── Scenario Summary (Base/Bear/Bull/Policy)
├── Monte Carlo Input Distributions (FA01 only)

SHEET 10: DASHBOARD / SUMMARY
├── Key Metrics (IRR, NPV, Payback, DSCR min, Avg)
├── Charts: Cash Flow Waterfall, Revenue Stack, Debt Service
├── Assumption Highlights
└── Risk Flags (Red/Amber/Green)
```

### 12.1.3.2 Color Coding & Modeling Discipline

| Color | Meaning | Examples |
|-------|---------|----------|
| **Blue** | Hardcoded Input (Assumption) | Capacity, Tariff, Capex, Interest Rate |
| **Black** | Calculation (Formula) | All formulas referencing inputs |
| **Green** | Link from Another Sheet | =Technical!B10, =Assumptions!C5 |
| **Red** | External Link / Warning | =['MarketData.xlsx']Sheet1!B2 |
| **Grey** | Inactive / Deprecated | Old scenarios, placeholder |

**Modeling Rules:**
| Rule | Enforcement |
|------|-------------|
| **No Hardcodes in Calculation Sheets** | Only Assumptions sheet has blue cells |
| **One Formula per Row/Column Block** | Copy across; no unique formulas per period |
| **Named Ranges for Key Inputs** | WACC, CarbonPrice_Base, Capex_Total |
| **Units in Column Header** | "Generation (MWh)", "Revenue (INR Cr)" |
| **No Merged Cells in Calc Areas** | Breaks copy-paste, auditing |
| **Error Checks Row** | Each calc sheet: balance checks, zero checks |
| **Version Control** | v1.0, v1.1... with change log |

### 12.1.3.3 Assumption Documentation Template

**Each Key Assumption Must Have:**
```
ASSUMPTION: [Name, e.g., "Solar Module Degradation"]
VALUE: [Base case value, e.g., "0.7%/yr"]
SOURCE: [Specific: "NREL 2023 Benchmark; Manufacturer warranty 0.5%/yr"]
RANGE: [Low / High for sensitivity, e.g., "0.5% / 1.0%"]
```

### Practical Exercise: Model Architecture Review

**Scenario:** You are reviewing a financial model for a 100 MW Wind project (ACM0002) submitted for investment committee. The model has these issues:

**Issues Found:**
1. Carbon price ($18/t) hardcoded in Revenue sheet (Row 45), not in Assumptions
2. Degradation formula: Year 1 = 19.5%, Year 2 = Year1*0.993, Year 3 = Year2*0.993... (unique formula per row)
3. PPA tariff escalation: 2% hardcoded in Year 2, 3% in Year 3, 2% in Year 4... (no pattern)
4. DSCR calculation: (PAT + Dep + Interest) / (Principal + Interest) — but PAT includes interest income
5. WACC = 10% used for NPV, but Cost of Equity = 14% used for Equity IRR (inconsistent)
6. Buffer pool (15%) applied to ERs AFTER carbon revenue calculation (double-counting revenue)
7. No error check row on Cash Flow sheet
8. Assumption "Carbon Price" has source "Market" (no specific citation)
