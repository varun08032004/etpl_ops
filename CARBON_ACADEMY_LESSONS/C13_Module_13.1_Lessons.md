# C13: Indian Carbon Market & CCTS
## Module 13.1: CCTS Framework & Obligations (3 lessons × 40min = 2h)

### Lesson 13.1.1: CCTS Architecture: PAT, CCTS, CCC
**Lesson Code:** C13.1.1
**Duration:** 40 minutes
**Lesson Type:** document
**Tier:** india_ether_track

**Learning Objectives:**
1. Explain the Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS) architecture and its evolution from PAT (Bloom: Understand)
2. Distinguish between Carbon Credit Certificates (CCC), PAT Escerts, and international credits (Bloom: Analyze)
3. Map the institutional framework: BEE, CERC, ICMS, DNA, exchanges (Bloom: Apply)

**Prerequisites:** C02.2.1 (Market Types), C10.1.1 (Registry Types)

**Why This Matters:**
India's CCTS is the world's largest emerging compliance carbon market. Understanding its architecture — how it builds on PAT, how CCCs differ from Escerts and international credits, and who governs what — is essential for any carbon professional operating in or with India. This is not theoretical: compliance deadlines are live, and the market infrastructure is being built now.

**Core Concept: CCTS as PAT Evolution — Compliance Market with Voluntary Layer**

### 13.1.1.1 From PAT to CCTS — The Evolution

**Perform, Achieve, Trade (PAT) — The Foundation (2012-2023):**
| Aspect | PAT Scheme |
|--------|------------|
| **Legal Basis** | Energy Conservation Act 2001 (amended 2010) |
| **Administrator** | Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) |
| **Mechanism** | Energy savings targets (toe) for Designated Consumers (DCs) |
| **Tradable Unit** | Energy Savings Certificates (Escerts) — 1 Escert = 1 toe |
| **Cycles** | PAT Cycle I (2012-15), II (2016-19), III (2019-22), IV (2022-25), V (2022-25) |
| **Compliance** | DCs meet target or buy Escerts; penalties for shortfall |
| **Registry** | PAT Registry (BEE) |
| **Exchange** | IEX, PXIL (Escert trading) |

**CCTS — The Expansion (2023 Onwards):**
| Aspect | CCTS (Carbon Credit Trading Scheme) |
|--------|-------------------------------------|
| **Legal Basis** | Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act 2022; CCTS Notification 2023 |
| **Administrator** | BEE (Scheme Administrator) + CERC (Market Regulator) |
| **Mechanism** | GHG emission intensity targets (tCO2e/t output) for Obligated Entities |
| **Tradable Unit** | **Carbon Credit Certificates (CCC)** — 1 CCC = 1 tCO2e |
| **Scope** | Obligated Entities (compliance) + Voluntary entities (non-obligated) |
| **Linkage** | PAT continues for energy efficiency; CCTS for carbon |
| **International** | Article 6.2 ready; corresponding adjustment framework |

**Key Transition: PAT → CCTS**
```
PAT (Energy Efficiency)                    CCTS (Carbon Emissions)
├── Target: toe savings                    ├── Target: tCO2e intensity reduction
├── Unit: Escert (1 toe)                   ├── Unit: CCC (1 tCO2e)
├── Sectors: Industry, Transport, Buildings├── Sectors: PAT sectors + Power, new sectors
├── Compliance: Energy intensity           ├── Compliance: GHG emission intensity
└── Registry: PAT Registry                 └── Registry: ICMS (Indian Carbon Market System)
```

### 13.1.1.2 CCTS Institutional Architecture

| Institution | Role | Key Powers |
|-------------|------|------------|
| **Ministry of Power (MoP)** | Policy oversight | Scheme notification; sector coverage; target methodology |
| **Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE)** | **Scheme Administrator** | Target setting; methodology approval; ICC registration; compliance monitoring; ICMS oversight |
| **Central Electricity Regulatory Commission (CERC)** | **Market Regulator** | Exchange recognition; trading rules; settlement; surveillance; penalties |
| **National Designated Authority (DNA)** | **Article 6 Focal Point** | LoA issuance; corresponding adjustment; international transfer authorization |
| **Indian Carbon Market System (ICMS)** | **Central Registry** | CCC issuance, holding, transfer, retirement, surrender; account management |
| **Power Exchanges (IEX, PXIL, NSE, BSE)** | **Trading Platforms** | CCC trading; price discovery; clearing & settlement |
| **Accredited Verifiers (VVBs)** | **Verification** | Project validation/verification; ICC registration |
| **Central Electricity Authority (CEA)** | **Technical Standards** | Grid emission factor; methodology technical review |

### 13.1.1.3 Credit Types in India — CCC vs Escert vs International

| Feature | **CCC (CCTS)** | **Escert (PAT)** | **International (VCU/GS/CCER)** |
|---------|----------------|------------------|----------------------------------|
| **Unit** | 1 tCO2e | 1 toe (energy) | 1 tCO2e |
| **Scheme** | CCTS (Carbon) | PAT (Energy) | VCS/GS/CDM/CORSIA/CCER |
| **Registry** | ICMS | PAT Registry | Verra/GS/CDM/ICMS (bridged) |
| **Compliance Use** | CCTS surrender | PAT surrender | CCTS (if bridged + CA); CORSIA; Voluntary |
| **Voluntary Use** | Yes (BRSR, voluntary claims) | Limited | Yes |
| **Article 6.2** | CA required for export | No | CA required |
| **Vintage** | Annual (compliance cycle) | PAT cycle | Project vintage |
| **Methodology** | BEE-approved | BEE-approved | VCS/GS/CDM/CCER |
| **VVB** | BEE-accredited | BEE-empanelled | Verra/GS/CDM accredited |

**Bridging Pathways:**
```
INTERNATIONAL → ICMS:
Verra/GS/CDM Project
    │
    ├── Validation/Verification (VVB)
    │
    ├── ICC Registration (BEE)
    │
    ├── Corresponding Adjustment (DNA LoA) — IF international transfer
    │
    └── ICMS Issuance → CCC (with metadata: origin, vintage, methodology, CA status)

PAT → CCTS:
Escert (PAT Registry)
    │
    ├── Conversion methodology (BEE) — toe → tCO2e
    │
    └── ICMS → CCC (vintage mapped to PAT cycle)
```

### 13.1.1.4 CCTS Notification 2023 — Key Provisions

| Provision | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Scheme Commencement** | Phased: Compliance (Obligated Entities) first; Voluntary parallel |
| **Obligated Entities** | Notified by MoP (sectors + threshold); linked to PAT DCs initially |
| **Target Setting** | Emission intensity (tCO2e/unit output); trajectory to 2030 |
| **Compliance Cycle** | Annual (Financial Year: Apr-Mar); Surrender deadline: **May 31** (post-FY) |
| **CCC Issuance** | Verified projects → ICMS → CCCs (vintage = compliance year) |
| **Trading** | Recognized exchanges; T+1 settlement; position limits |
| **Banking/Borrowing** | Banking allowed (future compliance); Borrowing restricted |
| **Penalties** | Environmental compensation (per tCO2e shortfall); escalating |
| **Voluntary Participation** | Any entity can open ICMS voluntary account; buy/retire CCCs |

### 13.1.2.2 Target Setting — Emission Intensity Trajectory

**Target Methodology (BEE):**
```
EMISSION INTENSITY TARGET (tCO2e/unit output) = 
    Baseline Intensity × (1 - Annual Reduction Rate) ^ Years from Baseline

WHERE:
- Baseline Intensity = Avg emissions (tCO2e) / Avg output (units) over baseline years
- Annual Reduction Rate = Sector-specific (e.g., 2-4%/yr for industry; 3-5% for power)
- Trajectory = Aligned to India's NDC (45% emission intensity reduction by 2030 vs 2005)
```

### 13.1.2.3 Compliance Calculation — Step by Step

**Step 1: Determine Baseline Intensity**
```
Baseline Intensity = (Total GHG Emissions Baseline Years) / (Total Output Baseline Years)
- Emissions: Scope 1 + Scope 2 (per BEE methodology)
- Output: Physical production (tonnes cement, MWh, tonnes steel, etc.)
```

**Step 2: Calculate Annual Target**
```
Target Year N = Baseline × (1 - r)^N
r = sectoral annual reduction rate
```

**Step 3: Measure Actual Intensity (FY)**
```
Actual Intensity = (Verified Emissions FY) / (Verified Output FY)
- Emissions: Verified by accredited VVB per BEE methodology
- Output: Verified production data
```

**Step 4: Calculate Obligation**
```
IF Actual ≤ Target: COMPLIANT → Surplus CCCs = (Target - Actual) × Output
IF Actual > Target: NON-COMPLIANT → Deficit CCCs = (Actual - Target) × Output
```

**Step 5: Surrender CCCs**
```
Surrender Quantity = Deficit CCCs (rounded up)
- Surrendered from ICMS Obligated Entity Account
- Deadline: May 31 post-FY
- Banking: Surplus CCCs → Banked for future years
```

### 13.1.2.4 Banking, Borrowing & Penalties

| Mechanism | Rule | Strategic Implication |
|-----------|------|----------------------|
| **Banking** | Surplus CCCs carried forward indefinitely | Build buffer in low-cost years; sell later |
| **Borrowing** | Limited: up to 10% of target; must repay next year with interest | Emergency only; costly |
| **Penalty (Environmental Compensation)** | ₹10,000-50,000/tCO2e (escalating); plus compliance still required | Cost of non-compliance >> CCC price |
| **Carry-Forward of Deficit** | Not allowed (must surrender) | No rolling deficit |

**Penalty Escalation (Indicative):**
| Year of Default | Penalty Rate |
|-----------------|--------------|
| 1st Year | ₹10,000/tCO2e |
| 2nd Consecutive | ₹25,000/tCO2e |
| 3rd+ Consecutive | ₹50,000/tCO2e + potential regulatory action |

### 13.1.2.5 Verification Requirements for OEs

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **VVB Accreditation** | BEE-empanelled (not just Verra/GS); sector-specific scope |
| **Verification Frequency** | Annual (per compliance cycle) |
| **Verification Scope** | Emissions (Scope 1+2), Output, Emission Intensity, Monitoring Plan compliance |
| **Report Submission** | To BEE + ICMS by Apr 30 post-FY |
| **Verification Opinion** | Positive / Conditional / Negative (blocks CCC issuance/surrender) |

### 13.1.3.1 CCTS Trading Infrastructure — Exchanges & Settlement

**Recognized Exchanges (CERC-Recognized):**
| Exchange | Status | CCC Products | Settlement |
|----------|--------|--------------|------------|
| **IEX (Indian Energy Exchange)** | Recognized | Spot CCC; Future CCC (planned) | T+1 |
| **PXIL (Power Exchange India)** | Recognized | Spot CCC | T+1 |
| **NSE / BSE** | Pipeline | Spot CCC; Derivatives (planned) | T+1 |

**Trading Parameters (CERC Regulations):**
| Parameter | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| **Lot Size** | 1 CCC (1 tCO2e) minimum; standard lot = 1,000 CCCs |
| **Tick Size** | ₹1/tCO2e |
| **Price Bands** | Daily: ±10%; Annual: no circuit breaker |
| **Position Limits** | Client-level: 5% OI; Member: 15% OI; Client-level position limits per entity |
| **Trading Hours** | 9:15 AM – 3:30 PM (aligned with power market) |
| **Settlement** | T+1 (funds + CCCs); DvP via exchange clearing corp |
| **Margins** | SPAN-based + Extreme Loss Margin (ELM) |
| **Trading Hours** | Mon-Fri, 9:00-15:30 (aligned with power market) |

**Market Segments:**
| Segment | Participants | Use Case |
|---------|--------------|----------|
| **Compliance Segment** | Obligated Entities (surrender) | Surrender deadline: May 31 |
| **Voluntary Segment** | Any ICMS account holder | BRSR, voluntary claims, ESG |
| **Intermediary** | Brokers, traders, aggregators | Liquidity provision, arbitrage |
| **Article 6.2** | Authorized entities | ITMO export/import |

### 13.1.3.2 ICMS Registry — CCC Lifecycle Management

**Account Types in ICMS:**
| Account Type | Holder | Purpose | Key Features |
|--------------|--------|---------|--------------|
| **Obligated Entity Account** | Notified OEs | Compliance surrender; CCC holding | Mandatory for OEs; auto-linked to BEE ID |
| **Voluntary Account** | Any entity (corporate, individual) | Voluntary buying/retirement; BRSR claims | No compliance obligation |
| **Project Developer Account** | Registered projects | CCC issuance receipt; primary sale | Auto-credited upon issuance |
| **Exchange Clearing Account** | IEX/PXIL/NSE/BSE | Trade settlement; DvP | Segregated per member |
| **Bridge/International Account** | DNA-authorized entities | Article 6.2 ITMO import/export | CA tracking; corresponding adjustment |
| **Buffer/Buffer Pool** | BEE/ICMS | Reversal coverage | System-managed; auto-draw on reversal |

**CCC Lifecycle in ICMS:**
```
ISSUANCE → ACTIVE (Project Dev Account)
    ↓ TRANSFER
ACTIVE (Holding/Voluntary/OE Account)
    ↓ RETIREMENT (Voluntary claim / BRSR)
RETIRED (Immutable)
    ↓ SURRENDER (Compliance)
SURRENDERED (Immutable; compliance record)
    ↓ CANCELLATION (Regulatory)
CANCELLED (Immutable; quality control)
```

**Key Registry Functions:**
| Function | Description | API/Access |
|----------|-------------|------------|
| **Account Opening** | KYC + BEE verification; digital signature | Portal + API |
| **CCC Issuance** | Verified project → ICMS → Project Dev Account | Automated post-verification |
| **Transfer** | Account-to-account (DvP via exchange or bilateral) | API + Portal |
| **Retirement** | Claim specification (BRSR, voluntary, etc.) | Portal + API |
| **Surrender** | OE Compliance Account → BEE | Portal (OE only) |
| **Cancellation** | Quality control / regulatory | Admin only |
| **Reports** | Holdings, transactions, positions | Portal + API (real-time) |

### 13.1.3.3 India-Specific Bridging Considerations
