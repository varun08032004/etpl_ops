## 1. Pilot Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
Validate the CA-2026 EtherTrack Carbon Academy curriculum, delivery platform, assessment methodology, and certification pipeline with a representative internal employee cohort before full organisational rollout.

### 1.2 Scope
- **Programme:** CA-2026 EtherTrack Carbon Academy (full mandatory curriculum)
- **Curriculum:** 16 courses, 49 modules, 147 lessons, 119.5 total hours
- **Tracks:** Common Core + 5 specialist tracks (Carbon Ops, Engineering, Compliance, Finance, Product)
- **Duration:** 20 weeks (5 months) at 7 hours/week
- **Cohort Size:** 10-15 employees across 5 role categories

### 1.3 Pilot Phases

| Phase | Timeline | Duration | Focus |
|-------|----------|----------|-------|
| **Phase 0: Pre-Pilot** | Week -2 to 0 | 2 weeks | Recruitment, onboarding, baseline assessment |
| **Phase 1: Common Core** | Week 1-10 | 10 weeks | C01-C05, C13-C15 (Common Mandatory Core) |
| **Phase 2: Specialist Tracks** | Week 11-18 | 8 weeks | Role-specific tracks (parallel) |
| **Phase 3: Capstone** | Week 19-20 | 2 weeks | C16 Capstone + final assessment |
| **Phase 4: Evaluation** | Week 21-22 | 2 weeks | Feedback, analysis, go/no-go decision |

## 9. Pilot Support & Incident Process

### 9.1 Issue Classification

| Priority | Definition | Examples | Response | Resolution |
|----------|------------|----------|----------|------------|
| **P0 Critical** | System unusable | Auth down, data loss, LMS inaccessible | 15 min | 2 hours |
| **P1 High** | Major feature broken | Programme inaccessible, progress lost, assessment unavailable | 1 hour | 8 hours |
| **P2 Medium** | Feature degraded | UI bug, download fail, minor data issue | 4 hours | 24 hours |
| **P3 Low** | Cosmetic/minor | Typos, UI polish, non-blocking | Next sprint | Best effort |

### 9.2 Support Workflow

```
Employee reports issue
        ↓
Slack #training-support / Email training-support@ethertrack.in
        ↓
Triage by Training Ops (15 min P0, 1hr P1)
        ↓
Assign to: Platform Eng / Training Eng / Content Team
        ↓
Fix → Test → Deploy
        ↓
Confirm with reporter → Close
```

### 9.3 Escalation Matrix

| Priority | Primary | Secondary | Executive |
|----------|---------|-----------|-----------|
| P0 | Platform Eng Lead | Engineering Manager | CTO |
| P1 | Training Eng Lead | Platform Eng Lead | VP Eng |
| P2 | Training Eng | Training Ops | - |
| P3 | Training Ops | Training Eng | - |

### 9.4 Support Hours
- **Core Hours:** Mon-Fri 9:00-18:00 IST
- **P0 Coverage:** 24/7 on-call rotation
- **Pilot Period:** Extended to 8:00-20:00 for first 4 weeks
