## 4. Workload Analysis Summary

### 4.1 Recommended Configuration

| Parameter | Value | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| **Weekly Hours** | 7 hrs/week | Balances completion rate with workload |
| **Pilot Duration** | 20 weeks | Covers all tracks + buffer |
| **Pilot Calendar** | 2026-09-07 to 2027-01-25 | Accounts for holidays/buffer |

### 4.2 Weekly Time Budget (7 hrs/week)

| Activity | Hours | Frequency |
|----------|-------|-----------|
| Instructional (video/reading) | 3.5 | Weekly |
| Practical Exercises | 2.0 | Weekly |
| Assessments/Quizzes | 1.0 | Bi-weekly |
| Discussion/Application | 0.5 | Weekly |
| **Total** | **7.0** | **Weekly** |

### 4.3 Workload Risk Assessment

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| 7 hrs/week unsustainable | Medium | High | Protected time policy; flexible deadlines |
| 119.5 hrs too much for 16 weeks | Medium | High | Extended to 20 weeks |
| Capstone (15 hrs) compressed | High | Medium | Schedule final 4 weeks |
| Assessment burden | Low | Medium | Spread across weeks |
